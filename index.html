Dunes of the Zagros

A lightweight RTS inspired by classic base-and-squad warfare.

Scenario Lore

In a distant future where desert storms swallow satellites and memory alike, the fractured basin of Iranya sits atop shimmering Aether Brine—a psycho-reactive fuel that powers shields, fleets, and propaganda.

The Union of Atlantic States (UAS) enters Iranya under the banner of “stability logistics.” Its loud, theatrical Chancellor, Magnus Vhal, promises "victory so massive the dunes themselves will applaud" from gilded command balconies.

Opposing them, the Coalition of the Crescent Steppe blends militia, engineers, and nomad houses to resist occupation while competing for control of sacred wind-cities. Every convoy, refinery, and speech reshapes the war.

How to Play

  • Left click: select friendly unit.
  • Right click: move selected unit.
  • T: train an infantry unit at your Citadel (cost 50).
  • Y: train a skimmer tank (cost 120).
  • R: build a brine refinery near your Citadel (cost 150).
  • Destroy the enemy Citadel to win.
